How it works

  1. 01

    30 years of history

    We count how often it rained at your place within a week of your date, in each of the last 30 years.

  2. 02

    Blended with the forecast

    Within 16 days of the event, the live forecast gradually takes over from the climate record.

  3. 03

    A clear decision

    We compare your expected loss with the price of protection, uncertainty included: protect, don't protect, or close call.

A transparent method, explained step by step.

Plan B is made by Dorian Girod, MSc Statistics student at ETH Zurich. The data, the formulas and their limits are set out in a short methodology note.
